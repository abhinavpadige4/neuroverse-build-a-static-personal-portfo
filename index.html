About

Behind the Lens

I'm Arjun — a street photographer based between Mumbai and Berlin. For the past decade I've been wandering city streets with a camera, chasing light, gesture, and the fleeting moments that make urban life feel alive.

My work has been featured in Aperture, Monocle, and Le Monde, and hangs in private collections across four continents.
